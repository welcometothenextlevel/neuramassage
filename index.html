The NEURA Method

Classical massage was where it started. Not where it stops.

NEURA is Vlad’s own methodology. It takes the precision of the Swiss classical school and adds what he learned working with athletes under heavy physical and mental load: biomechanics, and how the nervous system holds on to stress.

The result is one long, unhurried session with a single aim: to give the body a full restart. It is built for people who never really switch off, and who carry it in their neck, their back and their sleep.

  1. 01

    Muscles

    Deep, precise work, zone by zone, on the tension you have stopped noticing. Pressure is set to you: firm if you like it firm, softer if you don’t.

  2. 02

    Nervous system

    Slow rhythm, warmth and time. The session is long on purpose, so the body has room to drop out of alert mode instead of just relaxing on the surface.

  3. 03

    Environment

    Heat, scent, light and sound, arranged in your own space, so nothing pulls you back: no reception desk, no changing room, no drive.

A session, step by step

From the doorbell to the last candle.

  1. Before

    Tell Vlad what your body needs

    A WhatsApp message is enough: where you hold tension, any health conditions, a pregnancy. Home visits are paid upfront by TWINT, bank transfer or card, so nothing interrupts the session itself.

  2. 10–15 min early

    He arrives and sets up

    Table, heated cover, fresh linens, oils, candles, music: ready in 10 to 15 minutes. Take a shower, ask questions, or watch from the sofa. All you need is a free 3 × 2 m space, ideally in a warm room around 24 °C.

  3. Before you lie down

    He steps out while you settle in

    You change in private, lie on the table and cover yourself with the blanket he has prepared. What you wear is your choice: your own underwear, disposable underwear he provides, a modesty cover, or nothing at all.

  4. 1.5 to 2.5 hours

    One area at a time

    Only the area being worked on is uncovered; everything else stays draped. Pressure is adjusted as you go. You can stop the session at any moment, without giving a reason.

  5. Afterwards

    He leaves. You stay.

    Vlad steps out again while you get up, then packs everything away quietly. What stays behind is a light scent of lavender, and an evening you don’t have to drive home from.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to prepare anything for your arrival?

Absolutely nothing. I arrive “turnkey”: a professional table with a wide mattress, warmed oils and premium aromatherapy. All you need is a quiet place and these two hours of rest for yourself. If you can make the room around 24 °C, that’s ideal, but not mandatory.

What exactly happens during the session? Will it hurt?

When I arrive, you show me where to set up. Setting up takes 10 to 15 minutes, and you can ask questions or share anything I should know. Many people take a shower or simply watch from the couch. When everything is ready I leave the room, so you can change and cover yourself with the blanket I’ve prepared. Then I come back and we begin. At the end I leave again so you can get up and dress, then I pack up and go.

Pressure is always adjusted to you. Some clients like it strong, most prefer it softer; tell me, and I adapt.

How does clothing work? I saw videos where the client looked undressed.

Your comfort is the foundation of the NEURA Method, so you choose: disposable underwear I provide, a special professional modesty cover (first-time clients always smile when I show it to them), your own clothes, or no underwear. I work on the “technical access” principle: only the zone that needs attention is open, while every other part of the body stays securely covered with a towel, also when moving from one zone to the next. You are always protected.

Honestly, I’m anxious about a male therapist coming to my home when I’m alone.

I understand completely. You are inviting a stranger, and a man, into your space. That’s why in the NEURA Method, professional distance is a baseline that cannot be broken. I arrive with ready-made equipment, create a calm atmosphere, do the session and leave you in a state of peace. Before every session I tell clients that the massage can be stopped at any moment without explaining the reason. It has never happened, but the option is always there.

I feel self-conscious that I’m not in “ideal shape”.

I work with your nervous system and your body, not with aesthetics. On the table you are a person who came for restoration and a moment of peace. There is no judgment, only care for how you feel. You don’t even need to think about hair removal or any personal preferences outside the usual standards; none of it affects the quality of the session.

How can I relax at home with chores all around?

That is the point of the method. Once the space is set, with the right sounds, scents, warmth and a professional approach, your home stops being a place of chores for those two hours. It becomes your personal spa. Even people who live in 24/7 multitasking mode manage to switch their head off.

Why do you mainly work with women?

I work with men as well, and the Classic Massage is for everyone. The NEURA Method itself was created specifically for women: female physiology and psycho-emotional state call for a more sensitive approach, and the method is built around creating a space of safety and trust.

Is the massage you offer erotic?

No.

The NEURA Method is a strictly therapeutic, clinical-grade service built on absolute respect. Any inappropriate behaviour ends the session immediately, without a refund.

Is it covered by Swiss health insurance (ASCA / RME)?

I am a certified therapist with ASCA accreditation in classical massage, which confirms my professional competence to Swiss standards. The NEURA Method, however, is a specialised signature treatment that goes far beyond a standard 50-minute classical massage: a 1.5 to 2.5 hour deep recovery session. It is a private-pay service and is not covered by insurance. Clients choose it for the depth and the lasting results, not the reimbursement.

Where do you travel?

Home visits in Geneva and Bern and their surroundings. Vaud, Zurich and Basel are possible on request; send a message with your address and preferred date.

Can I book while pregnant?

Yes. The Prenatal Massage (45 min) uses a dedicated pregnancy pillow and is adapted to your stage of pregnancy. Please tell me in advance that you are pregnant, and if you have specific medical concerns, check with your doctor before booking.

What if I’m running late, or need to cancel?

I arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to set up. If you are delayed, the session may be shortened to respect the next client’s time. To cancel or reschedule, please give at least 24 hours’ notice; later cancellations are charged 50% of the session price. Also let me know in advance about any acute health condition or fever.
